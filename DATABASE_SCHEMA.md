# TrendScout Studio — Relational Database Schema

The database implements all 35 required tables with primary keys, foreign key constraints, timestamps, status columns, source provenance, and query indexes.

## Core Tables

1. **`users`**: `id`, `email` (UNIQUE), `password_hash`, `name`, `role`, `created_at`, `updated_at`
2. **`user_settings`**: `id`, `user_id` (FK `users.id`), `discovery_prefs_json`, `monitoring_prefs_json`, `ai_prefs_json`, `notification_prefs_json`, `production_prefs_json`, `created_at`, `updated_at`
3. **`connected_sources`**: `id`, `name` (UNIQUE), `platform`, `enabled`, `config_json`, `quota_used`, `quota_limit`, `last_scan_at`, `status`, `error_message`, `created_at`, `updated_at`
4. **`source_runs`**: `id`, `source_id` (FK `connected_sources.id`), `status`, `items_found`, `new_discoveries`, `started_at`, `completed_at`, `error_message`
5. **`source_metrics`**: `id`, `source_id` (FK `connected_sources.id`), `entity_type`, `external_id`, `metric_type`, `metric_value`, `metadata_json`, `recorded_at`
6. **`people`**: `id`, `canonical_name`, `slug` (UNIQUE), `category`, `country`, `summary`, `biography`, `birth_date`, `image_url`, `underdog_classification`, `research_status`, `created_at`, `updated_at`
7. **`person_aliases`**: `id`, `person_id` (FK `people.id`), `alias_name`, `source_provenance`, `created_at` (UNIQUE on `person_id, alias_name`)
8. **`events`**: `id`, `title`, `description`, `event_date`, `category`, `location`, `source_url`, `source_name`, `created_at`, `updated_at`
9. **`person_events`**: `id`, `person_id` (FK `people.id`), `event_id` (FK `events.id`), `role`, `achievement`, `created_at`
10. **`discoveries`**: `id`, `person_id` (FK `people.id`), `event_id` (FK `events.id`), `source_id` (FK `connected_sources.id`), `headline`, `achievement`, `source_url`, `publisher`, `first_detected_at`, `trend_stage`, `independent_coverage_count`, `research_status`, `status`, `created_at`, `updated_at`
11. **`trend_observations`**: `id`, `discovery_id` (FK `discoveries.id`), `person_id` (FK `people.id`), `trend_stage`, `views_total`, `views_per_hour`, `views_per_day`, `channel_relative_perf`, `independent_coverage`, `coverage_growth_rate`, `engagement_score`, `momentum_score`, `saturation_score`, `observed_at`
12. **`videos`**: `id`, `person_id` (FK `people.id`), `discovery_id` (FK `discoveries.id`), `external_video_id` (UNIQUE), `platform`, `title`, `channel_name`, `channel_subscribers`, `url`, `views`, `likes`, `comments`, `views_per_hour`, `views_per_day`, `channel_relative_multiplier`, `published_at`, `recorded_at`
13. **`social_posts`**: `id`, `person_id` (FK `people.id`), `discovery_id` (FK `discoveries.id`), `platform`, `external_post_id` (UNIQUE), `author`, `content`, `url`, `upvotes_or_likes`, `comments_count`, `shares_count`, `published_at`, `recorded_at`
14. **`news_articles`**: `id`, `person_id` (FK `people.id`), `discovery_id` (FK `discoveries.id`), `title`, `url` (UNIQUE), `publisher`, `author`, `summary`, `published_at`, `retrieved_at`
15. **`research_sources`**: `id`, `person_id` (FK `people.id`), `url`, `title`, `publisher`, `published_at`, `retrieved_at`, `source_type`, `reliability_score`, `raw_excerpt`, `created_at`
16. **`research_claims`**: `id`, `person_id` (FK `people.id`), `source_id` (FK `research_sources.id`), `category`, `claim_text`, `verification_status` (`Verified`, `Reported`, `Attributed`, `Interpretation`, `Unverified`, `Unknown`), `event_date`, `contradicts_claim_id`, `confidence_score`, `notes`, `created_at`, `updated_at`
17. **`person_timelines`**: `id`, `person_id` (FK `people.id`), `claim_id` (FK `research_claims.id`), `source_id` (FK `research_sources.id`), `year_or_date`, `chronological_order`, `phase` (`Origins`, `Early Struggles`, `Setback/Rejection`, `Turning Point`, `Breakthrough`, `Recent Victory`), `title`, `description`, `verification_status`, `created_at`
18. **`underdog_assessments`**: `id`, `person_id` (FK `people.id`), `discovery_id` (FK `discoveries.id`), `classification`, `origins_summary`, `early_ambitions`, `disadvantages`, `rejections_and_failures`, `obstacles`, `turning_points`, `career_progression`, `recent_breakthrough`, `contradictions_report`, `research_gaps_report`, `audience_interest_report`, `assessed_at`, `updated_at`
19. **`opportunity_scores`**: `id`, `discovery_id` (FK `discoveries.id`), `person_id` (FK `people.id`), `overall_score`, `underdog_strength`, `transformation_score`, `achievement_significance`, `audience_momentum`, `documentary_potential`, `timeliness_score`, `evidence_confidence`, `saturation_risk`, `score_rationale_json`, `calculated_at`
20. **`watchlists`**: `id`, `user_id` (FK `users.id`), `person_id` (FK `people.id`), `discovery_id` (FK `discoveries.id`), `notes`, `alert_on_stage_change`, `created_at`
21. **`notifications`**: `id`, `user_id` (FK `users.id`), `discovery_id` (FK `discoveries.id`), `project_id`, `type`, `severity`, `title`, `message`, `action_url`, `metadata_json`, `is_read`, `emailed_at`, `extension_notified_at`, `dedupe_key` (UNIQUE), `created_at`
22. **`research_jobs`**: `id`, `person_id` (FK `people.id`), `discovery_id` (FK `discoveries.id`), `status`, `depth`, `sources_collected`, `claims_verified`, `contradictions_found`, `gaps_found`, `started_at`, `completed_at`, `error_message`, `created_at`
23. **`documentary_projects`**: `id`, `person_id` (FK `people.id`), `discovery_id` (FK `discoveries.id`), `title`, `slug` (UNIQUE), `status`, `target_duration_minutes`, `visual_style`, `aspect_ratio`, `image_interval_seconds`, `thumbnail_concept`, `youtube_titles_json`, `youtube_description`, `music_suggestions`, `created_at`, `updated_at`
24. **`documentary_scripts`**: `id`, `project_id` (FK `documentary_projects.id`), `current_version`, `title`, `opening_hook`, `full_narration_text`, `word_count`, `estimated_duration_seconds`, `status`, `approved_at`, `created_at`, `updated_at`
25. **`script_versions`**: `id`, `script_id` (FK `documentary_scripts.id`), `version_number`, `sections_json`, `full_narration_text`, `word_count`, `estimated_duration_seconds`, `change_summary`, `created_by`, `created_at`
26. **`script_references`**: `id`, `script_id` (FK `documentary_scripts.id`), `section_index`, `paragraph_index`, `claim_id` (FK `research_claims.id`), `source_id` (FK `research_sources.id`), `citation_note`, `created_at`
27. **`visual_segments`**: `id`, `project_id` (FK `documentary_projects.id`), `script_id` (FK `documentary_scripts.id`), `segment_number`, `segment_code`, `start_seconds`, `end_seconds`, `duration_seconds`, `narration_passage`, `image_prompt`, `character_ref_id` (FK `character_references.id`), `location_ref`, `generation_status`, `image_asset_id` (FK `image_assets.id`), `created_at`, `updated_at`
28. **`character_references`**: `id`, `project_id` (FK `documentary_projects.id`), `person_id` (FK `people.id`), `character_name`, `role`, `age_range`, `appearance_description`, `hair_description`, `clothing_description`, `body_proportions`, `locations_description`, `environmental_refs`, `visual_style`, `color_palette`, `master_prompt`, `reference_image_path`, `created_at`, `updated_at`
29. **`image_assets`**: `id`, `project_id` (FK `documentary_projects.id`), `segment_id`, `character_ref_id`, `file_path`, `file_url`, `prompt_used`, `provider`, `width`, `height`, `status`, `created_at`
30. **`audio_assets`**: `id`, `project_id` (FK `documentary_projects.id`), `segment_id`, `title`, `file_path`, `file_url`, `provider`, `voice_id`, `duration_seconds`, `transcript_text`, `is_master_narration`, `status`, `created_at`
31. **`subtitle_assets`**: `id`, `project_id` (FK `documentary_projects.id`), `audio_asset_id` (FK `audio_assets.id`), `format`, `file_path`, `file_url`, `cue_count`, `content_text`, `created_at`
32. **`production_exports`**: `id`, `project_id` (FK `documentary_projects.id`), `export_type` (`package_zip`, `video_mp4`, `google_flow_pack`), `file_path`, `file_url`, `file_size_bytes`, `manifest_json`, `video_assembled`, `status`, `created_at`
33. **`background_jobs`**: `id`, `job_type`, `queue_name`, `priority`, `payload_json`, `idempotency_key` (UNIQUE), `status` (`queued`, `running`, `completed`, `failed`, `dead_letter`), `attempts`, `max_attempts`, `next_run_at`, `locked_by`, `locked_at`, `heartbeat_at`, `result_json`, `error_message`, `started_at`, `completed_at`, `created_at`, `updated_at`
34. **`audit_logs`**: `id`, `user_id`, `actor`, `action`, `entity_type`, `entity_id`, `details_json`, `ip_address`, `created_at`
